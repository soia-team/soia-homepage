FAQ

Three answers before you choose.

Why is private delivery not one fixed price?

Materials, hosts, permissions, acceptance, and maintenance vary. We confirm the work before quoting instead of hiding those differences in a generic tier.

Is the open ecosystem really free?

Generic Skills in the public repositories are available under their open-source licenses. Models, host software, and external platforms may have their own terms or costs.

What does the ¥980 course price include?

The first-cohort course shape shown here: lessons, replay, learner exchange, and ongoing updates. Timing and places follow the current course page.
