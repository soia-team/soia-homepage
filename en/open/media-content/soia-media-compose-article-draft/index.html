WHAT IT DOES

A focused capability with a visible boundary.

Turn distilled user viewpoints and owned source notes into an article draft ready for channel-specific adaptation.

  • Starts from a natural-language outcome and the minimum required input.
  • Loads the canonical public instructions only when the intent matches.
  • Reports artifacts, checks, blockers, and any decision still owned by the user.

DELIVERY CONTRACT

What you should expect to receive.

INPUT

Clear starting material

A file, URL, repository, workspace, or goal that you are authorized to use.

PROCESS

Bounded execution

The smallest reliable steps, with previews and approval gates where risk increases.

RECEIPT

Reviewable outcome

Changed files, generated artifacts, validation results, limitations, and next actions.
