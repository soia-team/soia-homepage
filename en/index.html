FAQ

A few important questions, answered first.

The open ecosystem, course, and private services each have their own page and boundary.

How is SOIA different from a prompt?

A prompt usually describes one request. A Skill also defines when it applies, its steps, tools, boundaries, outputs, and acceptance checks. Multiple Skills can then form a rerunnable workflow.

Do I need all three hosts?

No. Choose whichever of Claude Code, Codex, or WorkBuddy fits your work. Each public capability documents its supported hosts and installation path.

Where is the line between open capabilities and private services?

Reusable methods, Skills, and tools are open where practical. Your accounts, data, templates, and proprietary processes stay private. Services cover adaptation, composition, acceptance, and maintenance without adding client materials to public repositories.

Will SOIA publish or take high-risk actions on its own?

Not by default. Public workflows preserve human approval; publishing, authorization, payment, and other external writes require user confirmation.
