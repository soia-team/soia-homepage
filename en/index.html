FAQ

A few important questions first.

Open ecosystem, products, course, and private services each have their own boundary.

What is the difference between the ecosystem and products?

The ecosystem is a free public catalogue of capabilities you can browse and install. Products package capability as a Skill, Workflow, Plugin, or Expert; private products are scoped after review.

Do products have one fixed price?

The open ecosystem is ¥0. Course pricing is pending first-cohort confirmation. Private Workflows, Plugins, Experts, and registries are scoped by materials, hosts, acceptance, and maintenance.

Do I need all three hosts?

No. Start with Claude Code, Codex, or WorkBuddy. Cross-host distribution is an additional validation scope.

Will my SOP or credentials become public?

No. Generic methods can be open; client materials, SOPs, templates, passwords, tokens, and cookies remain private and are held by the client in official channels.
