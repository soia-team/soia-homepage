BOUNDARY

Open what is reusable.
Private what is yours.

Generic capability stays reviewable and reusable. A client’s process, data, and credentials never enter a public repository.

PUBLIC / OPEN

What is public

  • Generic Skill instructions, scripts, and references
  • Install paths, version state, and public evidence
  • Reusable methods, templates, and documentation
  • Source that can be inspected and contributed to

PRIVATE / DELIVERY

What stays private

  • Client SOPs, templates, data, and proprietary rules
  • Private Skills, Plugins, Experts, and access configuration
  • Workflow combination, acceptance, enablement, and maintenance
  • Passwords, tokens, and cookies held by the client in official channels
